## Product Purpose

Users provide 来源 and 主题, read 知言 reports, direct a 立言文章, and submit articles toward publication destinations. Product terminology follows CONTEXT.md.

## Capabilities and Constraints

来源知言报告 and 主题知言报告 have different structures; use the current frontend components as authority. 主题 is optional, but the homepage must not discuss whether it is optional or mandatory and must not mention 提炼主题. Publication branches on the homepage illustrate the intended broader experience, not a promise that every destination is implemented.

One email OTP flow serves new and returning users. Homepage CTAs say 立即体验 while signed out and 前往工作台 while signed in. Existing access policy and workbench routes remain intact.

## Brand Commitments

立言阁 is a product of Nyquiste Corporation. Contact: birdyyao@nyquiste.com. Preserve the existing ivory, ink, muted brass, Chinese serif and sans-serif identity, language choices, and light/dark/system display modes. Call the display setting 模式 rather than 主题 on public pages.
