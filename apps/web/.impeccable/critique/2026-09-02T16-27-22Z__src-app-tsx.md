## Design Specificity Verdict

The interface is authored for LIYAN, not category-interchangeable. The signed-out experience has a strong Chinese editorial identity through the pagoda mark, Songti display type, warm ink-and-parchment palette, and restrained composition. The authenticated workbench is more generic: sidebar, tabs, cards, split panes, chips, and utility controls could belong to many AI document tools. The strongest opportunity is to turn 来源 → 主题 → 知言 → 立言 into a persistent, legible journey instead of hiding it inside two compound tabs.

The deterministic scan reported four warnings: two `side-tab` findings at `src/styles.css:2742` and `src/styles.css:2981` are false positives for semantic quotation treatments; `overused-font` at line 77 is low-signal because Inter is complemented by product-specific Chinese display typography; `layout-transition` at line 87 is a valid low-severity warning for animated `padding-left`. Browser inspection found the important issue the CLI missed: the fixed service alert overlaps theme and language preferences on narrow screens.

No reliable user-visible `[Human]` overlay remains. Injection succeeded and the console reported one anti-pattern, but visibility was unavailable from the isolated assessment thread. Screenshots, DOM geometry, console output, and source verification were used instead. The browser-only “transition: height” report was not reproducible in source or computed styles and is treated as a false positive.

## Overall Impression

LIYAN already feels serious, literate, and unusually trustworthy. Its best design work is not ornamental: it is the careful handling of unsaved work, paid actions, immutable publication snapshots, and uncertain external outcomes. The single biggest opportunity is orientation—making the product’s four-stage transformation visible and focusing each screen on one next safe action.

## What’s Working

1. **Exceptional safety and recovery design.** Unsaved work is guarded, failures preserve user input, destructive actions require confirmation, and publication distinguishes safe retry from unknown outcome.
2. **Coherent editorial visual language.** Warm neutral surfaces, restrained gold, Songti headings, compact sans-serif controls, and limited decoration suit a serious Chinese writing product.
3. **Strong implementation discipline.** Semantic landmarks, labels, focus management, reduced-motion handling, comprehensive light/dark tokens, and explicit workflow states are present. The focused accessibility suite passed 8/8 tests.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Loading can be presented as “task not found”

- **Location:** `src/components/AppShell.tsx:603`
- **Why it matters:** Direct links and reloads briefly tell users that important work does not exist while it is still being fetched.
- **Fix:** Add a distinct loading state; render not-found only after a settled 404, and give other failures a retry state.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] The four-stage product journey is hidden inside compound tabs

- **Location:** `src/components/TaskCard.tsx:179`
- **Why it matters:** First-time and interrupted users must remember what gates what, where reports live, and why 立言 is unavailable.
- **Fix:** Add a compact persistent stage spine—来源, 主题, 知言, 立言—with state and next safe action. Preserve the two-pane work layout beneath it.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Fixed service status obscures mobile preferences

- **Location:** `src/App.tsx:247`, `src/styles.css:1150`, `src/styles.css:1200`
- **Evidence:** At 416 px, the banner occupied x=104–313 while preferences occupied x=234–397; at 355 px, the ranges were x=89–267 and x=173–336.
- **Why it matters:** Theme and language controls become difficult or impossible to tap precisely when the service is degraded.
- **Fix:** Create one responsive top stack, place preferences below the alert, or move the alert into normal flow on narrow screens.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Theme choice forms a six-path decision cluster

- **Location:** `src/components/ThemeChoice.tsx:123`, `src/components/TaskCreationSession.tsx:497`
- **Why it matters:** Three candidates, freeform input, regeneration, and creation compete while the user is deciding whether to spend credits. Aspirational copy adds pressure instead of clarity.
- **Fix:** Explain optionality and consequence first, reveal candidates only after generation, and replace “打破信息茧房，取其精华，去其糟粕” with concrete outcome and cost language.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Publication decision and audit evidence share one long view

- **Location:** `src/components/PublicationCenter.tsx:216`
- **Why it matters:** Draft choice, preview, target confirmation, history, trace IDs, attempts, and raw evidence dilute the highest-stakes action and increase anxiety.
- **Fix:** Create a focused review → confirm → result arc. Collapse history by default and move trace/evidence behind a technical-details disclosure.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Touch targets are systematically undersized

- **Location:** `src/styles.css:140`, `src/styles.css:234`, `src/styles.css:1161`, `src/styles.css:2690`
- **Why it matters:** 28–42 px controls increase mistaps for touch and motor-impaired users, especially in the editor toolbar.
- **Fix:** Preserve compact visuals while extending transparent hit areas to at least 44×44 px.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P3] Sidebar collapse animates layout

- **Location:** `src/styles.css:87`
- **Why it matters:** Animating `padding-left` forces layout during the transition and may stutter with dense task content.
- **Fix:** Use a transform-based rail/content transition or contain the layout change.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable optimize`

### [P3] Focus-ring colors bypass theme tokens

- **Location:** `src/styles.css:1184`, `src/styles.css:1701`, `src/styles.css:2347`, `src/styles.css:2670`, `src/styles.css:2730`
- **Why it matters:** Repeated hard-coded accent RGB values can drift from future themes.
- **Fix:** Introduce an accent-focus token with light and dark values.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable document`

## Cognitive Load and Emotional Journey

The source-count limit, numbered source spine, one-at-a-time editor expansion, and explicit disabled reasons contain intrinsic complexity well. The overload appears at transitions: after theme generation there are at least six visible paths; a publication draft list can become unbounded; and an opened task exposes rename, delete, two compound tabs, plus version/source controls before the work begins.

The sign-in page is the emotional high point: calm, literate, confident, and exclusive without being theatrical. The service-unavailable banner creates an immediate mobile valley. Long-running and failed work is handled reassuringly. Publication is semantically careful, but the surrounding history and raw evidence dilute the completion peak.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-time writer:** Cannot infer the four-stage model from two compound tabs. The optional 主题 card does not plainly answer whether a theme is needed or what leaving it empty changes, so Jordan may spend credits defensively.
- **Alex, high-volume editor:** Gets persistent navigation but no visible shortcuts or repetitive-work acceleration. Rename/delete occupy the primary task band while frequent work actions remain deeper in each pane.
- **Mei, risk-averse publisher:** Receives excellent locked-snapshot and outcome language, but must scan selection, preview, target fields, history, attempts, and response evidence together. The screen feels like an operator console before a consequential action.

## Minor Observations

- Source edits save on blur without a lightweight “已保存” acknowledgment.
- Rename/delete are duplicated between sidebar menus and the task header.
- Theme switching cycles Light → Dark → System; the current value is visible, but the next click’s outcome is not predictable.
- Inter, Songti SC, and Noto families are not bundled, so brand character and spacing vary by operating system.
- The live environment exposed only the signed-out visual state; authenticated-workbench conclusions are source- and test-backed.
